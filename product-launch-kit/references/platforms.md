# Platform field patterns

Use these as a planning checklist. Confirm current limits and accepted formats on the destination before submitting because platforms change their forms and policies.

## Product Hunt

- Product name and tagline: concise, differentiated, outcome-led.
- Topics: choose the closest real categories; avoid keyword stuffing.
- Thumbnail/logo: provide a square mark with light/dark contrast and safe padding.
- Gallery: usually a cover plus a small sequence of product screenshots or video; caption each image with the user benefit.
- Maker profile: who you are and why you built it.
- First comment: personal context, problem, what changed, invitation for feedback, and a transparent launch goal.

## BetaList

- One-line pitch, longer startup description, launch URL, founder/company details, category, and product stage.
- Highlight what is new and who should try it now; state whether access is public or waitlisted.

## AlternativeTo / SaaSHub / generic directories

- Product name, canonical URL, short summary, full description, category, tags, platforms, integrations, pricing model, screenshots, logo, and alternatives.
- Keep factual compatibility claims explicit (for example, “web app” or “Chrome extension”) and avoid implying integrations that are only planned.

## AppSumo-style deal pages

- Lead with the use case and concrete deliverables.
- Separate current features from roadmap items; include support, refund, limits, and pricing details only when verified.

## Asset handoff conventions

Return an asset table with:

| Asset | Recommended treatment | Source/fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | Square mark, transparent PNG/SVG, light/dark variants | Existing favicon or generated brief |
| Cover | Wide hero composition, one readable headline, product UI focal point | Existing OG image or screenshot composition |
| Screenshot 1 | First-value moment | Capture from primary route |
| Screenshot 2 | Core workflow | Capture the interaction that differentiates the product |
| Screenshot 3 | Result/integration | Show outcome, export, or collaboration |
| Demo | 20–45 second silent or captioned walkthrough | Record primary workflow |
