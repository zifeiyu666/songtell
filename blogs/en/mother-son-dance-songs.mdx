# Mother-son dance songs that feel personal

The best mother-son dance song is not necessarily the most famous one. It is the song that gives the two of you a clear emotional place to stand for three minutes: grateful, funny, proud, or quietly close.

## Start with the feeling

- **Gratitude:** choose a lyric that notices the years of showing up.
- **Nostalgia:** look for a song that leaves room for childhood memories.
- **Joy:** a lighter rhythm can keep the dance from feeling overly solemn.
- **Pride:** pick a chorus that sounds like a promise rather than a goodbye.

## A short list to explore

1. “The Perfect Fan” — Backstreet Boys
2. “A Song for Mama” — Boyz II Men
3. “My Wish” — Rascal Flatts
4. “I Hope You Dance” — Lee Ann Womack
5. “93 Million Miles” — Jason Mraz
6. “Mama” — Il Divo
