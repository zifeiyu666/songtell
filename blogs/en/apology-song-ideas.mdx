# How to apologize in a song without making it performative

An apology song should not corner someone into forgiving you. It should make the truth easier to hear: what happened, what it changed, what you understand now, and what you will do differently.

## A four-part lyric shape

1. **Name the moment.** Be specific about what you did rather than hiding behind “we both got hurt.”
2. **Show the impact.** Describe what the other person had to carry.
3. **Take responsibility.** Do not add a “but” after the apology.
4. **Leave room.** A real apology does not demand an immediate answer.

## A simple prompt

Write three sentences beginning with: “I should have…”, “I understand now…”, and “I will…” Then turn only the most honest details into a verse and chorus. The song should support the apology, not replace a direct conversation.
